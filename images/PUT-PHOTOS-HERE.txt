TIPS
- iPhone photos are often .HEIC. Export or convert them to .jpg first.
- Resize to about 2000 px on the long side (around 300-600 KB each) so the site loads fast.
- If a photo is missing, the site shows a dashed box naming the exact file it expects.
- To change a photo's shape, edit style="--ratio: 3 / 2" on its .media__frame in index.html
  (e.g. 4 / 5 for portrait, 16 / 9 for wide). Add "--fit: contain" to avoid cropping.
